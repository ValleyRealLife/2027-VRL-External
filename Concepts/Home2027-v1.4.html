What to expect at VRL Kids

We're glad to walk with you through your child's spiritual growth. Every kid from birth through 5th grade checks in on our computer-based system, so you always know where they are and who they're with. First time? Stop by the New Family kiosk and we'll walk you through check-in and show you around.
