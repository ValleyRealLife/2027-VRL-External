# VRL Kids page: what's on it and what needs approval

The new VRL Kids page (new.vrl.church/ministries/kids) replaces www.vrl.church/ministries/kids. Most of the content comes from the current page. The staff photo is left out for now. Some new wording fills out the layout; it's marked below.

## How the page is laid out

1. **Top of the page.** The VRL Kids logo on the same green background as the Kids section of the homepage, a video (a placeholder for now) and a **Pre-register your kids** button.
2. **A verse and a welcome from the Kids team.**
3. **A bar that stays at the top of the screen as you scroll**, to jump to First visit, Classes, Playground or Milestones.
4. **Photos.** Six placeholders.
5. **Your first visit.** Three steps: pre-register, the New Family kiosk, and picking up your kids.
6. **Kids classes.** Early childhood and Elementary, each with its photo from the current page.
7. **Indoor playground.** A photo (placeholder) and the hours.
8. **Milestones.** Child dedication (with its photo from the current page) and VRL Kids baptism.
9. **Serve with VRL Kids.** A link to volunteer.

## Copy for approval

Each piece is marked:

- **Unchanged:** the same wording as the current page.
- **Edited:** shortened or tidied from the current page.
- **New:** written for this page.

### Kids classes

- **Introduction** (**new**, from the Location page): "Classes for birth through 5th grade happen during every service, with fully screened staff and volunteers."
- **Early childhood, birth to preschool** (**edited**): "These are fun and active stages in your child's life, and we care for your kids by providing a safe and fun place to grow. As parents lead their children to know God, we're here to support and resource every step."
