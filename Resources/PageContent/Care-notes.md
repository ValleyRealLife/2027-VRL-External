# Care page: what changed and why

The new Care page (new.vrl.church/ministries/care) replaces www.vrl.church/ministries/care. It keeps the same ministry and the same ways to get help, but reorganizes them so someone in a hard moment can act quickly. All the copy is below for approval.

## The short version

- **Asking for help is the first thing you see.** The top of the page has a **Request care** button. It opens the same Request care window as the link at the top of every page on the site.
- **Prayer requests moved up**, from the bottom of the page to just under the welcome.
- **The Care Center and VRL Compassion are now one thing: VRL Care.** People didn't need to know which team handles what. They need to know what help exists and how to ask for it.
- **Counseling with The Couch is shown as a partnership** in a panel beside VRL Care. It's a separate organization, so it's presented that way.
- **Photos.** The page has a set of photos (placeholders for now) so it feels personal, not like a list of services.

## Why these changes

**One place to ask.** The current page has two sections with two separate Request buttons (Request Care and Request Help) that go to the same form. Someone in crisis shouldn't have to work out which team they need. Now there's one VRL Care, one **Request care** button, and the care team routes it from there.

**Prayer is the lightest first step.** Many people aren't ready to ask for practical help but would welcome prayer. Moving prayer near the top gives them an easy way in. Its button opens the prayer form directly.

**Services grouped by what people need.** The two old lists overlapped (prayer, guidance and support appeared in both). They're now one set, in two groups:

- *Support and guidance:* conversations, prayer, and ongoing support.
- *Practical help:* visits, meals, help around the home, and help with bills.

**The Couch as a partner.** The Couch is a separate counseling practice that works out of The Care Center. Putting it in its own panel, labelled "Our partner", makes clear it isn't VRL staff. It also gives their contact details without mixing them up with VRL Care's.

## How "Request care" works

The **Request care** buttons on the page and in the utility bar at the top of every page open a small window with two choices: **Request help** and **Pray for me**. The **Request prayer** button goes straight to **Pray for me**.

**Not working yet:** the two forms inside that window haven't been built on the new site. Until they are, the window opens but the form inside won't load. The forms will be built once this content is approved.
