# VRL Students page: what's on it and what needs approval

The new VRL Students page (new.vrl.church/ministries/students) replaces www.vrl.church/ministries/students. Most of the content comes from the current page. MIDWEEK is gone: middle school now meets on Wednesdays (MSM) and high school on Sundays (HSM). The staff photo is left out for now. Some new wording fills out the layout; it's marked below.

## How the page is laid out

1. **Top of the page.** "VRL Students" on the same dark background as the Students section of the homepage, a video (a placeholder for now) and a **Find your night** button.
2. **A verse and a welcome.**
3. **A bar that stays at the top of the screen as you scroll**, to jump to Middle school, High school, Events or Next steps.
4. **Photos.** The worship photo from the current page, and five placeholders.
5. **Middle school.** MSM, with its Wednesday night shown as a card: the MSM logo, the next date and a link to the event. The card comes from the event in Rock, so the date stays current.
6. **High school.** The same for HSM and its Sunday night.
7. **Student events.** Upcoming events marked for Students in Rock, featured ones first, and a **See all student events** button that opens the events page already filtered to Students. MSM and HSM aren't repeated here.
8. **Next steps.** Student baptism, and serving with VRL Students.
