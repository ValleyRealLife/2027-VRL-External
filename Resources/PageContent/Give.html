Why we give

Trusting God with your money is part of growing as a follower of Jesus. How we manage what He's given us, our time, talents and finances, is one measure of spiritual maturity (Matthew 25:14–30).

Your tithes and gifts expand the Kingdom of God and are part of being a committed member of VRL. When you tithe and give through VRL, your generosity supports what God is doing in the Spokane area and around the world.

Questions

Giving is a step of faith, and questions are normal. Here are the ones we hear most. Don't see yours? Email us.

finance@vrl.church
Is my gift tax deductible?

Valley Real Life is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, so your gifts may be tax deductible depending on your situation. Check with your tax advisor to be sure.

Is my payment information safe?

We take it seriously. Valley Real Life doesn't store your payment details in any of our systems. Trusted payment partners who specialize in keeping them secure handle them. Want to know more? Email us.

Can I give to a specific campus or ministry?

Most gifts go into Valley Real Life's general fund. We do track giving by campus. If you'd like to support a specific ministry, email us. We'd love to hear your heart for it.

Do I need an account to give online?

No, you can give as a guest. With an account, your details are saved for next time, and you can see your giving history and year-end statements anytime.

Where do I get my giving statement?

We email every giver a thank-you at the end of the year. You can also download past statements or see this year's giving anytime on your Manage giving page (you'll need to sign in).

Something's wrong with a transaction

Email finance@vrl.church or call the church at 509-232-0840, and we'll help.

Are there classes on managing money?

We regularly host Financial Peace University by Dave Ramsey, a class on budgeting, saving and biblical stewardship. You can also watch our sermon series The Generous Life.

I have questions about tithing

We'd love to talk. Call the church at 509-232-0840 to set up a time with one of our pastors or staff.
