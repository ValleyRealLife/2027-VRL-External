# Discover VRL page: what's on it and what needs approval

The Discover VRL page (new.vrl.church/discover-vrl) is for people thinking about visiting for the first time. The header's **Discover VRL** button goes here. The content is the copy you sent, shortened for the web. A few lines are new; they're marked below.

## How the page is laid out

1. **Top of the page.** "New here?", the page title, the line from the page's Description, the "We are VRL" video and a **Plan your visit** button.
2. **Welcome.** "We're so glad you're thinking about visiting.", two short paragraphs and "Come as you are."
3. **A bar that stays at the top of the screen as you scroll**, to jump to What to expect, Questions, When you arrive, First Step Lunch or Directions.
4. **Photos.** Six placeholders.
5. **What to expect.** What a service is like, beside a photo.
6. **Questions.** Six questions that open when tapped, beside a photo.
7. **When you arrive.** Connecting Point (beside a map of the lobby) and the Café (with "Your first drink is on us").
8. **First Step Lunch.** What it is and when, beside the event's card (its photo, the next date and a **Save your seat** button), the same card the Students page uses for MSM and HSM.
9. **Directions.** A map, the address, service times and **Get directions**, beside ways to watch online. This matches the Location page.
