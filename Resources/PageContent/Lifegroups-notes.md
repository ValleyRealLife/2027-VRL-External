# Life Groups page: what's on it and what needs approval

The new Life Groups page (new.vrl.church/ministries/life-groups) replaces www.vrl.church/ministries/life-groups. The Life Groups team hasn't supplied copy yet, so most of the wording is new. It's drawn from the current page and from what we know about how groups work. Please review all of it.

## How the page is laid out

1. **Top of the page.** A video (a placeholder for now) and a **Find a group** button that jumps down to Join a group.
2. **A verse and why Life Groups matter.**
   Under it, a bar with **Start with Rooted**, **Join a group** and **Group resources** that stays at the top of the screen as you scroll, so you can jump between sections.
3. **Photos.** Six placeholders.
   **New to Life Groups?** Rooted, as the place to start: the Rooted logo with its next date and a sign-up button, linking to the Rooted event. It comes from the event in Rock, so the date stays current, and the section (and its link in the bar) disappears when Rooted has no upcoming date.
4. **Join a group.** Three ways in, each with a button to the group finder:
   - **On site:** groups that meet at VRL. People sign up and meet with the same group each week.
   - **Drop in:** groups anyone can come to any week, with no sign-up. Their days and times will come from the group finder.
   - **Off site:** groups in homes around the valley.
5. **Group resources.**
   - **This week's group questions:** found automatically from the latest message that has group questions in Rock, so it stays current with no weekly update.
   - **Links:** questions for past messages, and RightNow Media.
   - **A note for leaders:** a link to My groups in My Account.

## Copy for approval

Each piece is marked:

- **From the current page:** reworded from the existing page.
- **New:** written for this page.

### Top of the page

- **Title:** Life Groups
- **Line under the title:** the page's Description in Page Properties. It's set in Rock, not here.
- **Button:** Find a group

### Verse and welcome

- **Verse** (Hebrews 10:24–25, NIV; **new**, shortened for the large display): "Let us consider how we may spur one another on toward love and good deeds, not giving up meeting together…"
- **Welcome** (**from the current page**): "Community is an essential part of our faith. Life Groups are smaller groups that meet through the week to build friendships, care for each other, study the Bible and talk about how God's Word is working in our lives."
- **Tagline** (**new**): "Find your people."

### New to Life Groups?

- **Heading:** New to Life Groups?
- **Introduction** (**new**): "Start with Rooted, the easiest way to meet new friends and find your way into a group."
- **Card:** the Rooted event's photo, "Next" with its next date, and a *Sign up for Rooted* button. All from the event in Rock.

### Join a group

- **Introduction** (**new**; the group types come from the current page and finder): "There's a group for every stage and schedule: men's, women's, mixed, young adult and recovery groups, at VRL and in homes around the valley."
- **On site** (**new**): "Groups that meet at the Barker campus through the week. Sign up, and you'll meet with the same people each week." Button: *Find an on-site group*
- **Drop in** (**new**): "Come any week, no sign-up needed. Find one that fits your schedule and just show up." Button: *Find a drop-in group*
- **Off site** (**new**): "Groups that meet in homes and other places around Spokane Valley, on different days and times." Button: *Find an off-site group*

### Group resources

- **Introduction** (**new**): "Every week's message comes with questions for your group, so you can keep the conversation going."
- **This week's group questions:** the message title and date, with an *Open the questions* button. It's hidden in a week when no message has questions.
- **Links** (**new**):
  - "Questions for every past message"
  - "Video Bible studies on RightNow Media"
- **For leaders** (**new**, from "Leading a Life Group? Thank you!" on the current page): "Leading a Life Group? Thank you! Your groups, and the tools to manage them, are in My Account under My groups." Link: *Go to My groups*

## Questions for the Life Groups team

- **Group types:** are men's, women's, mixed, young adult and recovery still the right list?
- **Help finding a group:** the current page has a "help me find the right group" form. Should the new page offer that, or a contact?
